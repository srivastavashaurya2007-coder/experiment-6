Bioscope

We enjoyed seeing Bioscope, it consisted of photos of many famous personalities, movie posters and ancient kingdoms. In the background 70's music was also playing. The bioscope had lenses resembling a tiffin box, maybe they used steel tiffin boxes for it. Seeing the images were really entertaining.

Statues and art and craft

We saw many animal statues, including an elephant and a sculpture of a fly surrounded by waste. We saw many art stalls featuring beautiful Madhubani paintings of gods. The paintings reflected the effort and creativity of the artists. There were also wooden toys and toys inspired by superheroes and anime.
